[01]About

Born and raised in Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria, now based in Los Angeles, California, USA. Right from a young age, I’ve always been a tech enthusiast; I was always the kid who wanted to know what made things click and tick when it came to software, hardware, and people…lowkey.

I enjoyed setting up new systems at home and in school, be it new printers, monitors, or multimedia home setups, to setting up the wifi and creating user accounts for the new devices people around me got.

So when it came time to pick a career and what to study, it was no surprise that Computer Information Technology stood out to me; it is an intersection of software automation, hardware design, and operation, with a direct effect and interaction with individuals and businesses. And it was something I had experience in, and I find it interesting.

I have had a lot of “wow” moments in class, but one that I cannot forget was the day I sat in class and was playing around with physical machines that were on the other side of the world from my little terminal. Turning them on and off, and using what I want, when I want, if I see fit… that is where my focus in cloud computing started. As time went on, I learned how those machines affect people and businesses daily in ways I had never imagined, like Virtualization, IoT, Networking, and security.

What keeps me interested is how foundational Cloud infrastructure is. Most people never see it, but it sits behind nearly everything they use, and that role is only getting bigger. AI is a major reason for that, but it has also added to my curiosity, especially about what happens when you combine it with cloud Services like Lambda, EventBridge, and AWS Bedrock; you get systems that can react to events, interpret what’s happening, and help automate decisions instead of executing a predefined set of steps.

Now I simply focus on building and securing infrastructure in the cloud, where systems meet the people using them. I like learning how those systems are built, how they fail, and what it takes to keep them reliable and secure. This site is where I document what I build, break, and learn.

Currently

Currently, I am finishing up the final year of my bachelor’s degree, working in a remote cybersecurity externship program, studying for the next certification, and practicing skills by building projects that I find interesting, useful, and fascinating really. Oh yeah, and looking for a Job lol.
